# Hemant Rohera — three granted inventions

**Hemant Rohera** (Hemant Karamchand Rohera) is an engineer and inventor based in Pune, India. He founded Rohera Healthcare & Technology (eMedica) and Rohera Emerging Energies, and has more than 29 years of research and development experience in bioelectronics, energy storage and automotive power systems.

Archived on Zenodo: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24245995](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24245995).

This repository is a short, citable index of his three granted patent families, with their drawing sheets. Each section links to a fuller explanation on [hemantrohera.com](https://hemantrohera.com/).

## 2. Hybrid Power Pack — US 10,523,019 B2

The Hybrid Power Pack pairs two energy stores behind a battery management system, balancing circuits, a controller and a switch topology that includes a bidirectional switch between the stores. Its twenty-two figures describe fifteen numbered embodiments. They vary which stores are paired, how much is integrated into one chip, whether the bidirectional switch is present, and how the pack is charged.

![FIG. 2 of US 10,523,019 B2 by Hemant Rohera: Hybrid Power Pack first embodiment](https://hemantrohera.com/figures/patent-drawings/hemant-rohera-hybrid-power-pack-us10523019-fig02.png)

- Every figure explained: [Hybrid Power Pack patent drawings](https://hemantrohera.com/hemant-rohera-hybrid-power-pack-patent-drawings.html)
- Essay: [fifteen embodiments, one switched architecture](https://hemantrohera.substack.com/p/hemant-roheras-hybrid-power-pack)

## 3. OMNIA Drive — US 11,117,476 B2

OMNIA Drive is a vehicle power generating system. FIG. 1 shows the generators that can feed it, alone or in combination, FIG. 2 the power processing unit that decides how that power is used, and FIG. 3 the six-step method.
